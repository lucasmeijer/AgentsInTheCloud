# Demo Video

Make a video that feels like a person sitting next to the viewer, using the product and talking them through it.

- **Show real use.** Drive the product the way a normal user would, at a pace a viewer can follow. Cut dead time like loading and typing delays.
- **Narrate.** Write a short, conversational script that says what's happening and why it matters. Turn it into a natural-sounding voiceover with text-to-speech, and keep each line in sync with what's on screen.
- **Subtitle.** Burn the narration in as subtitles, so the video still works muted. Place them where they won't cover what matters or the player's controls.
- **Keep it short.** Show only what the viewer needs to get the point.
- **Check it.** Watch and listen to the result before handing it over. Fix anything out of sync, cut off, or hard to read.

When you're making several videos of the same thing, like a before and after, keep them in the same style so they're easy to compare.
