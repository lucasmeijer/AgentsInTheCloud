# Narrated Video

Make a video that feels like a person sitting next to the viewer, using the product and talking them through it.

- **Show real use.** Drive the product the way a normal user would, at a pace a viewer can follow. Cut dead time like loading and typing delays.
- **Narrate.** Write a short, conversational script that says what's happening and why it matters, in sync with what's on screen.
- **Voice it, if you can.** If the environment has text-to-speech credentials, turn the script into a natural-sounding voiceover. If not, skip the audio; the subtitles carry the narration alone.
- **Karaoke subtitles.** Burn the narration in as subtitles that highlight each word as it's spoken, or at a comfortable reading pace when there's no voice. Place them where they won't cover what matters or the player's controls.
- **Keep it short.** Show only what the viewer needs to get the point.
- **Check it.** Watch the result, and listen if there's a voice, before handing it over. Fix anything out of sync, cut off, or hard to read.

When you're making several videos of the same thing, like a before and after, keep them in the same style so they're easy to compare.
